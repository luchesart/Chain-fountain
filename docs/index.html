READ THE MOTION

What is the regulator doing?

Natural regulation

Gravity, transport and boundary forces select a natural height. Stability brings a small perturbation back to that height.

A chosen motion

The feedback law changes the pickup boundary force. Positive u brakes; negative u assists. A smooth reference asks for a different height.

Physical limits

Tracking is useful only while height, transport and tension remain admissible. Force clipping or actuator lag changes the ideal tracking dynamics.
